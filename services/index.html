FAQ

Questions, answered.

What tasks can you automate?

Anything repetitive and rule-based: phone and chat conversations, order and appointment handling, data entry, document processing, follow-ups and reporting. On our call we'll point out which of your tasks are the best fit.

Will an AI agent replace my staff?

Agents take over the repetitive volume so your people can focus on work that needs judgement and a human touch. Anything unusual is handed to your team.

Do I need to change the software I use?

Usually not. We connect agents to the tools you already use — your store, CRM, calendar, spreadsheets, email or messaging apps.

How long does a project take, and what does it cost?

It depends on the workflow. After a free discovery call we send a clear scope, timeline and price — and we're committed to delivering on time.
