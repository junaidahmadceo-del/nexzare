What it does

Hundreds of images. One click.

Bulk prompts

Load a .txt file with one prompt per line. Foxi Flow sends them to Flow one by one and waits for each result.

Images or videos

Pick the model, ratio, quality, length and number of outputs in the panel. Foxi Flow sets Flow to match and shows the models your own Flow account offers.

Start frames

In Frames mode each video gets its own Start image, plus an optional End image. Start stays locked until every prompt has its Start image.

Same character

Add up to 3 reference images, from your computer or your Flow uploads. They are attached to every prompt.

Auto-download

Every image or video is saved to your Downloads folder with a numbered name in prompt order (e.g. 001_Your_Prompt_Words.png), one folder per prompt file.

Runs in the background

Turn on “Run in background” and keep working in other tabs while Flow generates.

More features

  • Flow's Agent mode is switched off automatically, so prompts go straight to the prompt box.
  • Pause, resume, stop, and “start from prompt #”.
  • Automatic retries and one-click retry for failed prompts.
  • A reset button returns everything to the defaults.
  • Progress is saved, even if you close the panel.
How it works

Four steps to a folder of results.

  1. STEP 01

    Install Foxi Flow from the Chrome Web Store and open your project in Flow.

  2. STEP 02

    Click the Foxi Flow icon; the side panel opens next to Flow.

  3. STEP 03

    Choose Image or Video and your settings, load your prompt file, and press Start.

  4. STEP 04

    Your images or videos download automatically, one after another.

FAQ

Foxi Flow questions.

Does Pro include Flow credits?

No. Foxi Flow uses your own Flow account; Flow's own credits, limits and Google's terms still apply.

Do I need an API key or a Google Cloud account?

No. It works with the Flow website you are already signed in to.

Why does Chrome show “Foxi Flow started debugging this browser”?

Flow ignores clicks made by scripts, so Foxi Flow sends real mouse clicks and typing to your Flow tab only. That bar is Chrome telling you this is happening. It reads nothing else and stops when the work is done. Don't press Cancel on it while the queue runs.

Where do my prompts and images go?

They stay in your browser; only the licence key is checked with Gumroad. Read the Privacy Policy.

Is it the same subscription as Gemnix?

No. Each product has its own plan.

How do I cancel?

Use the link in your receipt email or "Manage subscription" in the extension.

Which browsers?

Google Chrome, version 116 or newer, on desktop.
