FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Gemnix?

Gemnix is a Chrome extension that turns a text file of prompts into a folder of finished images on Gemini. It sends your prompts one by one, can attach the same reference character to every prompt, and downloads every image automatically with a numbered name. You get 10 images free, then it is $2/month. See how Gemnix works.

What is Foxi Flow?

Foxi Flow is a Chrome extension that sends a text file of prompts to Flow one by one, makes images or videos, and saves every result automatically. You get 10 images or videos free, then it is $2/month. See how Foxi Flow works.

Do Gemnix and Foxi Flow need separate subscriptions?

Yes. Each product has its own $2/month plan and its own free trial.

Do I need an API key?

No. Gemnix uses the Gemini website you are already signed in to — no API key, no Google Cloud account and no setup.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your prompts, reference images and generated pictures stay in your own browser. The only thing that leaves it is your licence key, which is checked with our payment provider, Gumroad. There is no analytics or tracking. Read the Gemnix Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel?

Use the link in your receipt email or "Manage subscription" in the extension. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. More help is on the Gemnix Support page.

Do you build custom tools?

Yes. Alongside our own products, we design and build AI agents and automation for e-commerce brands, healthcare organisations and teams. Explore custom AI or book a call.
